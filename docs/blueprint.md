# **App Name**: UsahaLink

## Core Features:

- Business Data Entry Form: A user-friendly form for inputting comprehensive data about business actors, including company details, contact information, and business type.
- Google Sheets Data Integration: Seamlessly store and retrieve business actor data directly from a designated Google Sheet, acting as the primary database.
- Google Drive File Uploads: Allow users to upload and link relevant documents (e.g., permits, licenses) to each business actor record, stored securely in Google Drive.
- Business Data Listing & Search: Display a searchable and sortable list of all entered business actor data, enabling quick lookup and filtering.
- Data Editing & Deletion: Enable authorized users to edit existing business actor details and remove records from the system.
- AI Business Compliance Guide Tool: An AI-powered tool that, based on the entered business type and location, suggests a general checklist of potential compliance requirements.

## Style Guidelines:

- The visual design revolves around a professional and trustworthy aesthetic. The primary color is a dependable blue (#2266B3), chosen to evoke efficiency and clarity, ensuring excellent contrast with lighter elements. The background is a very light desaturated blue (#E7ECF0), providing a calm and clean canvas that minimizes eye strain. The accent color is a bright and vibrant green (#19E783), strategically used to highlight interactive elements and call-to-actions, ensuring they stand out while maintaining an analogous relationship to the primary blue for harmony.
- For optimal readability and a modern, objective feel, the application will use 'Inter' (sans-serif) for all text, suitable for both headlines and detailed body content such as forms and data tables.
- Use clear, vectorized icons with a minimalist, line-art style. Icons should represent common business concepts, document management, and data operations (e.g., 'edit', 'upload', 'search') to intuitively guide users through the interface.
- A clean, structured, and responsive layout that prioritizes content and usability. Forms will feature logical grouping of fields, clear labels, and ample white space. Data tables will be easy to scan, with adjustable columns and clear headers for efficient data consumption.
- Subtle and functional animations will be incorporated to provide feedback for user interactions, such as form submissions, data loading, and modal transitions. Animations should be swift and enhance the user experience without being distracting.
